# Shipping policy

## Delivery times

Orders are packed within **1 business day**. Delivery time after that depends
on the method you chose:

- **Standard shipping: 3 to 5 business days**
- **Express shipping: 1 to 2 business days**

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed
after 2 PM are packed the next business day.

## Shipping costs

- Standard shipping: **$4.99**
- Express shipping: **$12.99**
- **Free standard shipping on orders over $50** (before tax)

Free shipping applies to the order total after any discounts. If you return
part of an order and the remaining total falls below $50, the shipping cost is
not charged retroactively.

## Order tracking

When your order ships we email you a tracking number. Tracking usually starts
showing movement within **24 hours** of the email.

An order moves through these stages: **processing**, then **shipped**, then
**delivered**. You can ask support for the current status of any order using
its 4-digit order number.

## Cancelling before shipping

You can cancel an order **free of charge while it is still processing** — that
is, at any time before it ships. Cancelling is immediate and the refund follows
the normal timing of **5 to 7 business days**.

Once an order is marked as **shipped** it can no longer be cancelled. Wait for
it to arrive and then return it under the 30-day return policy.

## Delivery areas

We ship to street addresses and PO boxes nationwide. We do not currently ship
internationally.
