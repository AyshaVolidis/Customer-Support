# Damaged, defective or wrong item

## Report it within 14 days

If an item arrives **damaged, faulty out of the box, or is not what you
ordered**, tell us within **14 days of delivery**.

This is faster and more generous than a normal return: you pay nothing, and you
do not need the item to be unused or in its original packaging.

After 14 days a damaged or faulty item is handled under the **12-month
warranty** instead, and a wrong item under the **30-day return policy**.

## What to send us

Contact support with your **4-digit order number** and tell us what is wrong.

- **Damaged or defective item: photos are required.** Send 2 or 3 photos
  showing the damage, and one of the shipping box if it is also damaged.
- **Wrong item received: photos are not required.** Just tell us what arrived
  instead. A photo of the label helps but is optional.

## What you get

You choose one of:

- a **free replacement** of the same item, shipped by **express** at no cost,
  usually arriving in **1 to 2 business days** once we approve the claim, or
- a **full refund**, including the original shipping cost, taking **5 to 7
  business days** to reach your original payment method

If the item is out of stock we issue the refund and tell you when it is
expected back.

## Returning the faulty item

We email a **prepaid return label** within **1 business day** of approving the
claim. Return shipping is always free in these cases.

For low-value items we sometimes tell you to keep or dispose of the item. If so
we say that explicitly — otherwise please send it back within 14 days of
receiving the label.

## Timing

We review a report within **1 business day** and tell you the decision. A
replacement is shipped the same day it is approved, if the item is in stock.
