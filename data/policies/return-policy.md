# Return policy

## Return window

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery**. The 30 days are
counted from the day the order is marked as delivered, not from the order date.

Items bought during a sale can also be returned within 30 days. Gift cards and
downloadable products cannot be returned.

If your item arrived damaged, defective or wrong, do not use this policy — see
the damaged or wrong item policy, which has a shorter 14-day window and covers
the return shipping.

## Condition of the item

To be accepted, a returned item must be:

- unused and in the same condition you received it
- in its original packaging, with any tags still attached
- complete, including cables, chargers, manuals and free gifts

Items that show signs of use, missing parts or damage caused after delivery may
be refused, or refunded in part.

## How to start a return

1. Contact support with your **4-digit order number** and say which item you
   want to return and why.
2. We email you a **prepaid return label** within 1 business day.
3. Attach the label to the parcel and drop it off at any carrier location.

Return shipping is **free** for the first return of an order. For a second
return of the same order, a **$4.99** label fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing

Once your parcel reaches our warehouse we inspect it within **2 business days**.
After it is approved, the refund is issued to your **original payment method**
and takes **5 to 7 business days** to appear, depending on your bank.

We refund the price of the item and any tax paid. The original shipping cost is
refunded only if the whole order is returned.

## Exchanges

We do not process exchanges directly. Return the item for a refund and place a
new order for the one you want.
