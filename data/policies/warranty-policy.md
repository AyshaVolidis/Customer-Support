# Warranty policy

## Warranty length

Every item comes with a **12-month warranty** against manufacturing defects,
counted from the **delivery date**.

Accessories that are included free with a product (cables, cases, adapters)
carry a shorter **3-month warranty**.

The warranty stays with the order, so you do not need to register anything. We
look it up from your 4-digit order number.

## What is covered

- manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- a component that stops working during normal use
- a battery that holds less than 60% of its rated capacity within 12 months

If the fault is covered, we repair the item, replace it, or refund it — in that
order of preference, depending on what is available.

## What is not covered

- accidental damage, drops, cracked screens
- liquid or water damage, unless the product is rated as water resistant
- normal wear such as scratches, faded print or worn cables
- damage from using a charger or part not supplied by us
- any item that has been opened, modified or repaired by someone else
- loss or theft

An item that arrived already broken is **not** a warranty case — that is
covered by the damaged or wrong item policy, which is faster.

## How to claim

1. Contact support with your **4-digit order number** and describe the fault,
   including when it started.
2. Send **2 or 3 photos or a short video** showing the problem. For a fault
   that cannot be photographed, describe what happens and when.
3. We answer within **2 business days** with a decision and, if the claim is
   accepted, a prepaid shipping label.

Repairs take **7 to 10 business days** from the day we receive the item.
Refunds, where they apply, take **5 to 7 business days** to reach your original
payment method.
